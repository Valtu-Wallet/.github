# Getting help

- **Documentation:** <https://valtu.io/docs>, including the [integration guide](https://valtu.io/docs/integration) and the [API guide](https://valtu.io/docs/api).
- **Customers:** open **Support** in the dashboard (the team sees which workspace it's about), or email support@valtu.io.
- **Sales and partnerships:** <https://valtu.io/contact>.
- **Service status:** <https://status.valtu.io>.
- **Security issues:** never in public issues; email security@valtu.io (see SECURITY.md).
